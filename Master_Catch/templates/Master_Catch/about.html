About Master Catch

A personal-best fishing log for anglers who only care about the biggest fish of each species.

Most fishing apps log every catch. Master Catch keeps just one record per species, so your records page is always a quick list of your best fish. Each record saves the species, length, date and place it was caught, plus optional notes about bait or conditions.

Species you can log

Largemouth, smallmouth, white and striped bass, wiper, walleye, saugeye, crappie, bluegill, channel, blue and flathead catfish, northern pike, rainbow trout and common carp.
